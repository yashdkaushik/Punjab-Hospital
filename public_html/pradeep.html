Dr. Pradeep Kumar

Orthopaedics Specialist

Dr. Pradeep Kumar is a trusted Orthopaedic Specialist at Punjab Hospital & Trauma Centre, known for his precision in treating musculoskeletal disorders and joint-related conditions. With extensive experience in both surgical and non-surgical orthopaedic care, he ensures high-quality outcomes for patients suffering from fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, and spine-related issues.

He is well-versed in advanced orthopaedic procedures, including total joint replacement, arthroscopy, and trauma surgeries. Dr. Kumar’s patient-first approach emphasizes thorough diagnosis, modern rehabilitation protocols, and personalized care plans to support faster recovery and long-term mobility.

Dr. Kumar is a key contributor to the hospital’s mission of accessible and affordable orthopedic care, combining skill, compassion, and professionalism to serve patients across Saharanpur and surrounding regions.
