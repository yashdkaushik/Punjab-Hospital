Dr. Keval Sondager

MBBS, MD, DCH
Pediatrician

Dr. Keval Sondager is a dedicated and compassionate Pediatrician at Punjab Hospital & Trauma Centre, specializing in the medical care of infants, children, and adolescents. With qualifications including MBBS, MD, and DCH (Diploma in Child Health), Dr. Sondager is deeply committed to ensuring the healthy development and well-being of young patients.

He provides expert care in the management of childhood illnesses, immunizations, nutritional guidance, and developmental assessments. Dr. Sondager is known for his calm approach, clear communication with parents, and ability to connect with children in a comforting and reassuring way.

With a blend of academic expertise and clinical empathy, Dr. Keval Sondager plays a vital role in making Punjab Hospital a trusted center for pediatric care in the region.
