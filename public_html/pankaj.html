Dr. Pankaj Pawar

MBBS, MS – Orthopaedics
Gold Medalist (Delhi)

Trauma Surgeon | Internationally Trained

Dr. Pankaj Pawar is an esteemed Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgeon at Punjab Hospital & Trauma Centre. With a distinguished academic record as a Gold Medalist from Delhi and international training from Singapore and Germany, Dr. Pawar is widely recognized for his expertise in orthopedic trauma care and joint reconstruction.

His clinical excellence spans fracture management, arthroscopic surgeries, joint replacement, and complex trauma procedures. Dr. Pawar's international exposure ensures patients receive advanced, evidence-based treatment aligned with global best practices.

Known for his precision and compassionate approach, he blends modern surgical techniques with a focus on patient safety and rapid recovery. He plays a key role in elevating the standards of trauma and orthopedic services offered at Punjab Hospital.
