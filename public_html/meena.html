Dr. Meena Gupta

MBBS, MD, FRCOG
Senior Consultant – Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Dr. Meena Gupta is a highly accomplished Obstetrician and Gynaecologist at Punjab Hospital & Trauma Centre, bringing over two decades of clinical excellence to women's healthcare. She holds MBBS and MD degrees, along with the prestigious FRCOG (Fellowship of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists), signifying her global recognition in the field.

Dr. Gupta specializes in comprehensive women’s health — covering high-risk pregnancies, infertility, menstrual disorders, prenatal and postnatal care, and minimally invasive gynecological procedures. Her patient-centric approach, combined with her vast experience, ensures every woman receives respectful, safe, and evidence-based treatment.

With a commitment to continual learning and ethical medical practice, Dr. Meena Gupta has earned the trust of thousands of families. She is known not only for her clinical skills but also for her empathetic communication and personalized care plans.
