Dr. Sajan Kawatra

BAMS – General Physician

Dr. Sajan Kawatra is a skilled General Physician at Punjab Hospital & Trauma Centre, bringing years of experience in primary healthcare, patient diagnosis, and integrative treatment strategies. Holding a BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) degree, he effectively combines traditional and modern practices to provide holistic care.

As a frontline physician, Dr. Kawatra serves patients with acute and chronic illnesses, and plays an important role in the hospital’s outpatient services. He is deeply committed to preventive health, lifestyle counseling, and ethical medical practices that ensure trust and satisfaction.

Known for his patient-friendly nature and thorough approach to treatment, Dr. Sajan Kawatra is a respected member of the hospital’s medical team and contributes significantly to the hospital’s mission of affordable, accessible, and compassionate healthcare.
