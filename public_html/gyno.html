Dedicated to Women’s Health

Punjab Hospital offers expert gynecological care focusing on prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of women’s health conditions. Our specialists provide a warm, respectful, and private environment for patients at all life stages.

✔ Menstrual and hormonal disorders
✔ Reproductive health and infertility treatments
✔ Antenatal & postnatal care
✔ Menopause management

Pregnancy & Prenatal Services

Our obstetric team delivers personalized maternity care — from conception to childbirth — using modern diagnostics, ultrasonography, and safe delivery practices.

  • High-risk pregnancy management
  • Routine pregnancy checkups & vaccinations
  • Fetal growth monitoring & ultrasound
  • Painless delivery options
  • Post-delivery support & breastfeeding guidance

Your well-being is our priority — whether it’s reproductive health, family planning, or safe childbirth, we’re here for every step of your journey.
