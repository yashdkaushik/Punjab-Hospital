About Punjab Hospital

Punjab Hospital is dedicated to delivering high-quality healthcare with compassion, timeliness, and affordability. Feedback and suggestions from our patients help us continuously enhance our services.

Our goal is to be a trusted healthcare partner for every family, offering a wide range of specialized services in a caring and comfortable environment. Your health is our priority.

Our Quality Standards

  • Continuously monitor and evaluate treatments.
  • Align performance with national healthcare standards.
  • Focus on preventive care for better patient outcomes.

Certifications & Accreditations

  • NABH-Certified: Highest standards in clinical care and safety.
  • NABL-Accredited Lab: Precision diagnostics under ISO 15189.
  • NABH Emergency Standards: Rapid, life-saving emergency care.
