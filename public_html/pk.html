Dr. Varma P.K

General Physician

Dr. Varma P.K is a skilled and experienced General Physician at Punjab Hospital & Trauma Centre. With an academic background that includes an MBBS and MS, Dr. Varma brings broad-based medical knowledge and a disciplined surgical understanding to patient care.

He specializes in diagnosing and managing a wide range of general medical conditions, offering preventive care, treatment plans for chronic illnesses, and early detection of health issues. Dr. Varma is particularly known for his thorough consultations, accurate assessments, and personalized health advice for patients of all ages.

As a vital member of our multidisciplinary team, Dr. Varma is committed to delivering high-quality, accessible, and ethical care that addresses the everyday health needs of our community with empathy and medical excellence.
