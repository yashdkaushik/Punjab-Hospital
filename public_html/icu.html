ICU & NICU Services

Advanced Critical Care for All Ages, From Newborns to Adults

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Our state-of-the-art ICU at Punjab Hospital is equipped with cutting-edge life support systems and continuous monitoring equipment to manage critically ill patients with the utmost precision.

  • 24×7 intensive monitoring by trained ICU staff
  • Ventilator support and cardiac monitors
  • Infection control protocols in place
  • Dedicated ICU beds with isolation capacity
  • Multi-specialty intervention and consultation

Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU)

Our Level II NICU provides specialized care to premature babies, low-birth-weight infants, and critically ill newborns. Staffed by experienced neonatologists and nurses, it ensures round-the-clock care for your little ones.

  • Incubators, warmers, and neonatal ventilators
  • Phototherapy units and infusion pumps
  • Trained neonatal nurses and pediatricians
  • Safe, sterile, and quiet NICU environment
  • Parental counseling and regular updates

Both ICU and NICU departments at Punjab Hospital are committed to saving lives through precision medicine, compassion, and constant vigilance.
