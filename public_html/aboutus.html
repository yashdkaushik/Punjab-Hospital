About Punjab Hospital

Punjab Hospital is a leading multi-speciality healthcare provider in Saharanpur, committed to delivering safe, affordable, and advanced medical care to every patient. Our experienced team of doctors, nurses, and paramedical staff strive for excellence in every aspect of patient treatment.

Our Services

  • General Ward, Private Rooms & ICU with 24x7 Monitoring
  • Modular Operation Theatres equipped with Latest Surgical Equipment & Ventilators
  • Specialist Consultations (MBBS, MD, MS, FRCOG, DCH, etc.)
  • Advanced Diagnostics – Blood Tests, X-Ray, CT Scan, MRI, Ultrasound, ECG, Endoscopy, and more
  • 24x7 Emergency & Ambulance Support
  • Gynecology, Laparoscopic Surgery, Pediatrics, Orthopedics, Neonatal ICU (NICU), Onco Surgery & more
  • Insurance & Cashless Payments Accepted

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to offer personalized, affordable, and trustworthy healthcare to every patient who walks through our doors. Our vision is to become Punjab's most trusted hospital by upholding the highest standards of care, compassion, safety, and innovation.

Community Impact

Punjab Hospital is actively involved in free health camps, vaccination drives, and health education programs for the local community. Our periodic pediatric and gynecology camps ensure early detection and prevention of serious illnesses.

Our Departments & Facilities

  • General Ward & Private Rooms with 24/7 Nursing Care
  • Gynecology & Maternity Care – Normal Delivery & C-Section
  • Modular OT for Laparoscopic, Hernia, Gall Bladder, Fibroid Surgeries, etc.
  • ICU/NICU with Advanced Ventilator and Sterilized Anti-Microbial Setup
  • Dedicated Pediatric & Neonatal Intensive Care Units
  • Reception & Support Staff available 24/7 for Appointments & Inquiries
  • Diagnostic Lab with Digital X-Ray, ECG, Blood Tests & Pathology
